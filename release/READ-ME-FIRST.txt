fp EL Zone / False Color toggle + legends
SIGMA fp firmware Ver.5.02 ONLY (not the fp L, not other versions).
Unofficial, not affiliated with SIGMA. RAM only: nothing is written to flash.

INSTALL
1. In the camera: Menu -> Custom -> button settings, assign False Color to a
   button.
2. Copy AutoRun.txt to the root of the SD card (only one AutoRun.txt).
3. Camera off, battery out 10 seconds, battery in, start the camera.
   Wait for "fp ELZone/FC ready" the first time.

USE (on the button assigned to False Color)
  short press          colours on / off
  half-second hold     show or hide the legend while the colours are on
                       (a hold while off turns on colours and legend)
  The legend comes back as you left it; after each start it begins hidden.
  EL ZONE style shows the EL Zone legend, FALSE COLOR style the False Color
  legend. EL Zone labels beyond clipping are red: +4..+6 at ISO 100/200,
  +6 at ISO 400/3200.

REMOVE
Delete AutoRun.txt, take the battery out for 10 seconds, start again.
The power switch alone keeps the card in memory: always pull the battery
when removing it or changing to a different card.

If it freezes or glitches: power off, remove the battery and AutoRun.txt,
wait 10 seconds and restart without it.
